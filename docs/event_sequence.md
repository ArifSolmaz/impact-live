# Recommended public-event sequence for the AYAP-1 terminal impact (with contingencies)

Derived from the accompanying paper (in preparation). All times relative to the predicted impact epoch T0 at the Moon.
Light time to Earth: 1.19–1.35 s. Assumed telemetry/ground latency 5–60 s; broadcast production latency 10–60 s.

## Before the event (weeks → hours)
1. **Mission disclosure** (≥ 4 weeks before): orbit plane, predicted impact ellipse and epoch with uncertainties, whether
   guided braking will be attempted, descent-camera and downlink plan. Release the final tracking solution as CCSDS OEM/SPICE.
2. **Orbital tasking**: pre-impact LROC NAC reference images of the ellipse; coordination letters to the LRO project / LROC
   operator, KARI/KASA (Danuri LUTI) and ISRO (Chandrayaan-2 OHRC).
3. **Network rehearsal**: Tier-1 stations (NELIOTA; a Turkish professional station with a GPS-timed fast camera; a
   near-infrared station; a southern-hemisphere station) run the common pointing chart and the real-time pipeline on a
   natural-flash night and on the night before; injection–recovery on the actual equipment.
4. **Public briefing** framed as technical uncertainty: "a flash may be too faint for small telescopes; instruments X and
   Y will try; here is what will be known and when". State explicitly that a livestream without a visible transient is
   not an optical detection. Pre-register the engagement study (Appendix A of the manuscript) before this briefing.

## Live programme (T0 − 60 min → T0 + 60 min)
5. **Telemetry wall**: range, Doppler, altitude, predicted loss-of-signal countdown; explain the 1.3-s light time and the
   telemetry/broadcast latencies on screen.
6. **Onboard descent imagery** as received (frames before loss of signal).
7. **Dark-side telescope feeds** (Turkish station + NELIOTA) with the predicted position marked; no claim is made from a
   single feed.
8. **T0 + light time + latency: loss of signal** → telemetry confirmation of the impact (this is the first guaranteed milestone).
9. **T0 + 5–60 min: rapid processed replay** from the Tier-1 pipeline *only if* a two-station (or dual-camera) coincidence
   is found; otherwise an explicit statement "no optical detection yet; analysis continues; this was expected in the
   fainter half of the predictions".

## After the event
10. **T0 + 1–24 h**: consolidated result — confirmed detection with light curve and timing, or upper limits per station.
11. **T0 + 1–5 weeks**: orbital crater reveal (LRO NAC first pass 0.4–27 d after impact for a well-located site; public
    release typically 12–34 d after imaging) — presented as a second, distinct milestone; if no orbiter is available, a
    later survey image replaces it.
12. **T0 + ~6 months**: low-Sun morphology/DTM, payload science (LNT, radiometer, radiation), engagement-study results.

## Contingency branches (pre-written)
- **No optical detection** (modal outcome in the fainter half of the prior): run steps 8, 9 (negative), 10, 11.
- **Cloud at the Turkish station** (probability 0.30–0.68 by season): switch the feed to the clearest Tier-1 station; the
  Turkish venue becomes a watch party; record this as an analysis branch in the engagement study.
- **Far-side, limb or sunlit impact**: no optical event is promised; programme built on telemetry + crater reveal.
- **Loss of signal earlier/later than predicted**: report the observed LOS time; the pipeline searches ±10 min.
- **Orbiter unavailable**: replace step 11 with a later survey or a request to other orbiters; say so in advance.
